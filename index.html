the story

Hi, I'm Anakaren

It all started at a little workshop my friend Gera hosted in February 2025 — I made tiny donut earrings and instantly fell in love. Since then, clay has become my therapy, my creative outlet, and my joy.

I'm self-taught, based in Northern California, and obsessed with making pieces that make you say "oh how cute, I want this." Every piece is carefully researched and crafted with high-quality, tarnish-free, hypoallergenic materials.

🌸 polymer clay 🐚 freshwater pearls ✨ tarnish-free 🌿 hypoallergenic
